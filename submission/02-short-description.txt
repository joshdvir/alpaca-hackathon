Yield-paca is a multi-agent AI trading platform built on the Alpaca Trading API and Model Context Protocol (MCP). It pairs LLM-driven research, debate, and execution agents with deterministic risk gates, orchestrating three strategies (Mid-Band Movers, Overnight Reversal, and a Bull/Bear-Debate ticker pipeline) through Temporal.io in Ruby on Rails — running safely against live Alpaca paper-trading accounts.
